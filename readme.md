> *"Let us join hands in kindness and build our homeland together."*

**Sanatpoya** (meaning *Dynamic Industry* in Persian) is a comprehensive Iranian industrial platform that brings together specialists, consultants, manufacturers, and contractors — creating a professional space for knowledge exchange and service delivery.

## ✨ Features

- 👷 **Expert Directory** — Browse top industrial specialists and consultants by category
- 🛒 **Online Store** — Buy and sell industrial products professionally
- 📢 **Business Advertising** — Boost visibility on search engines
- 🎫 **Ticketing System** — Online support via ticket submission
- 👥 **Team Showcase** — Meet the people behind the platform
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Optimized for both mobile and desktop
- 🌐 **Full Persian (RTL) Support**
